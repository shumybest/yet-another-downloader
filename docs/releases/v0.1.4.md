# yet another downloader v0.1.4

This Intel macOS pre-release fixes incomplete YouTube downloads and adds a non-intrusive collapsible browser capture panel.

## Included

- Paste a YouTube video page into the desktop's manual-download form. Supported watch, short-link, shorts, embed and live video URLs are resolved through the pinned yt-dlp engine.
- Captured Googlevideo CDN tracks use their original YouTube page to download complete video and audio. CDN-only credentials and browser byte ranges are not forwarded to the page extractor.
- Audio-only or incomplete resources fail before conversion with clear guidance. Final merged output paths take priority over leftover audio, partial downloads and intermediate tracks; output validation remains mandatory.
- YouTube extraction failures retain their original error instead of falling back to downloading webpage HTML.
- The Chrome floating panel has Collapse/Expand controls. New captures and metadata updates only update counts while collapsed; switching videos does not expand it. Page reload resets it to expanded.
- Expanded resource lists scroll internally and occupy at most half the video height, capped at 320px.
- The desktop app, Chrome extension, pinned Python/yt-dlp engine, FFmpeg/ffprobe runtime, license notices, corresponding source and SHA-256 checksums are bundled.

## Upgrade

Download the DMG or ZIP and check `SHA256SUMS.txt`. Quit the old app from its macOS menu-bar menu before replacing it; closing its main window only hides it. Open the new app and use Help to prepare the bundled extension files, reload the unpacked extension in `chrome://extensions`, then refresh video pages.

Existing settings, history, port `8765` and the `m3u8bridge://` integration remain compatible. Proxy settings are retained; new installations have proxying disabled.

## Platform And Licensing

- macOS Intel `x86_64` only. Apple Silicon is not validated in this release.
- Ad-hoc signed, not Apple Developer ID signed or notarized. macOS may require opening the app through Finder's context menu.
- Project code is GPL-3.0-or-later; bundled components retain their own licenses. The matching corresponding-source archive and dependency manifests are supplied with this release.

## Verification And Limitations

- Source verification covers TypeScript, Python, Rust, desktop/extension builds and real Chromium overlay interaction tests.
- The reported YouTube video was sampled through a proxy at default 1080p and low-resolution settings: both 8-second samples merged to H.264/AAC MP4 and passed validation and full-sample decoding. This is not a full-length download or a native Quick Look UI test.
- Other ordinary webpages still require browser media capture. Protected or DRM-encrypted streams are not supported; downloads remain subject to source-site authorization and terms.
