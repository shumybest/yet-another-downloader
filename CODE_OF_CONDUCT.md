# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity and orientation, or technology choices.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Protect private information and avoid publishing credentials or private media.
- Respect licensing, authorship, and content-ownership boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained unconstructive argument.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Submitting proprietary, unlawfully obtained, or incompatibly licensed material.
- Encouraging misuse of the project to bypass access controls or violate rights.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options on the [shumybest GitHub profile](https://github.com/shumybest). Do not disclose sensitive incident details in a public issue.

Maintainers may edit, reject, remove, or ban contributions and participants whose behavior is inappropriate, threatening, harmful, or inconsistent with this policy. Enforcement decisions will consider impact, context, recurrence, and community safety.
